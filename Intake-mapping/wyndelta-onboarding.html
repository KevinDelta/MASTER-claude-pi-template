Projects & areas
Each project gets its own workspace with AGENTS.md and context files. Each area inside a project gets a full CONTEXT.md — Purpose, Functions, Workflow, Standards, Skills, and References — written from what you fill in here.
Projects live at ~/.openclaw/workspace/projects/<slug>/ and hold their own context and routing.
Areas live at projects/<slug>/areas/<name>/CONTEXT.md — each becomes a routing row and a complete context file.
