## 1. The Offer (Hormozi Grand Slam Framework)

### What WynDelta Actually Sells

You don't sell "AI consulting." You don't sell "automation." You sell a measurable EBITDA improvement in a supply chain operation — delivered through a structured, low-risk engagement that leaves the client owning deployed infrastructure.

That framing matters for pricing, content, and every conversation.

---

### The Grand Slam Offer: The ARA Sprint™

**Core Promise:**
> "We map your highest-impact supply chain processes, quantify the EBITDA opportunity, and deploy your first AI agent — all in 8 weeks. You see results in 90 days or we work for free until you do."

**What's Included:**

| Deliverable | What It Is | Perceived Value |
|---|---|---|
| Automation Readiness Assessment | Full process map of 3–5 workflows; pain scored, readiness scored | Clarity on where to start |
| Opportunity Priority Stack | Quantified EBITDA impact per process, ranked by composite score and risk | $10K consulting report equivalent |
| Agent Design Brief | Specific agent designed for your #1 process | Custom architecture spec |
| Deployed AI Agent | Fully operational agent running in your environment | The actual product |
| Structured Pilot (90 days) | Shadow → Assisted → Autonomous mode progression | Risk-free validation |
| PE-Ready Impact Report | EBITDA delta, FTE equivalents freed, automation coverage % — formatted for PE diligence | $5K standalone value |
| Client Ownership Handoff | Client team trained to own and iterate the system | No dependency on WynDelta to keep it running |

**Price Anchor Logic (Hormozi-style):**
- Even a conservative ARA engagement should identify $200K–$2M in addressable annual EBITDA improvement
- At a 6–10x EBITDA multiple, that's $1.2M–$20M in enterprise value impact
- A $25K–$45K engagement fee represents a 5–80x ROI on the value unlocked
- Frame accordingly: "We charge based on the value you're leaving on the table, not the hours we work"

**Recommended Pricing Structure:**
- **ARA Sprint:** $25,000–$40,000 (project-based, scope-defined)
- **Post-pilot Retainer:** $3,000–$5,000/month (support, iteration, second agent)
- **Done-With-You option:** Lower fee ($12K–$18K) for orgs that want to run the process internally with WynDelta facilitating — expands reach to smaller orgs

**Risk Reversal (make it a no-brainer):**
> "If the ARA doesn't surface at least $200K in addressable annual EBITDA opportunity, we refund 50% of the engagement fee."
*(This almost never triggers if you run the assessment correctly. But it removes the buyer's fear.)*

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary: Mid-Market Supply Chain Operators

**Profile:**
VP Operations, COO, or Owner/Operator at a supply chain-adjacent company. 20–250 employees. Feeling the pain of manual, human-dependent processes but don't have an internal AI team to do anything about it.

**Industries in priority order:**
1. Food & beverage distribution (highest manual process density, clear EBITDA pressure)
2. Third-party logistics (3PL) providers
3. Industrial distribution and wholesale
4. Light manufacturing with supply chain ops
5. Customs brokers and freight forwarders

**Their pain, in their words:**
- "We're spending 40 hours a week on manual data entry between systems that don't talk"
- "One person knows how our procurement process works and they're a flight risk"
- "Our margin is getting squeezed and we can't keep hiring to solve it"
- "We know AI is happening but we don't know where to start"

**What triggers the buy:**
- Pain is known and felt — they've been talking about "fixing this" for 12+ months
- Recent event: new PE backer pressure, a key employee left, a compliance near-miss
- Someone in their network described a similar win

---

### Secondary: PE Operating Partners

**Profile:**
Operating partner, VP of Operations, or portfolio performance lead at a PE firm with 3–15 portfolio companies in supply chain, distribution, industrials, or logistics. Managing hold-period value creation across multiple platforms.

**Their language:** EBITDA improvement, labor efficiency, scalability prior to exit, automation coverage, process standardization across portfolio

**Why they matter:** One PE relationship = access to 5–10 portfolio companies. You get introduced with warm credibility. They have budget specifically for operational improvement. Their timeline (3–7 year hold) aligns with a multi-phase engagement roadmap.

**Target PE segments:**
- Lower mid-market PE ($50M–$500M AUM) focused on industrials, distribution, logistics
- Family offices with operating company portfolios
- Independent sponsors doing roll-ups in supply chain verticals

## 4. Core Message Architecture

### The One-Sentence Positioning:
> "WynDelta deploys AI agents inside supply chain operations — so the work your team is doing manually gets done automatically, and the EBITDA shows up in 90 days."

### Message Hierarchy (answer these in every piece of content and every conversation):

**1. Why should I care?**
Every supply chain operation has 3–5 processes eating 20–40% of your team's time for work that should be automated. That's not a tech problem — it's a margin problem. The companies that move first on this will have a structural cost advantage that's hard to reverse.

**2. What is the solution?**
WynDelta runs a structured Automation Readiness Assessment, identifies your highest-EBITDA-impact opportunity, designs an AI agent for it, and deploys it in 8 weeks. You get a working system, not a report.

**3. Why WynDelta?**
Three things most AI consultants can't say: Kevin has run supply chain operations (understands the real workflows), has the technical depth to build and deploy agents (not just advise on them), and uses a proprietary methodology that quantifies EBITDA impact before any money is spent on deployment.

**4. What should I do?**
Book a free 30-minute ARA Discovery Call. No pitch. Kevin maps your top 2–3 manual process pain points and tells you honestly if automation is the right lever. If it is, you'll leave with a preliminary opportunity score.

## 5. Channel Strategy: LinkedIn-First Inbound

### Why LinkedIn is the right primary channel right now:
- Your buyers (COOs, VPs Ops, PE operating partners) are active here
- Kevin's background (ops + tech + consulting) is the exact positioning that earns organic shares
- Content scales; cold outreach hits volume limits
- A strong LinkedIn presence is the credibility signal that warms cold outreach later

### Kevin's Content Authority Pillars

**Pillar 1 — Operational Reality (40% of content)**
Content written from inside the supply chain, not outside. Specific, textured, shows you've been there.
- "Here's what actually breaks when your freight reconciliation runs on spreadsheets"
- "The procurement workflow I saw at 3 distribution companies — and why they all had the same failure point"
- "Manual data entry is not a people problem. It's an architecture problem."

**Pillar 2 — AI Without Hype (30% of content)**
Clear, honest, specific. No buzzwords. Builds trust with skeptical operators.
- "What an AI agent actually does inside a supply chain process (step by step)"
- "3 supply chain tasks where AI adds immediate ROI. 3 where it's not ready yet."
- "Why most AI pilots fail in operations — and the 4 things that make them stick"

**Pillar 3 — EBITDA Framing (20% of content)**
Speak the language of value creation. This is the bridge to the PE audience.
- "How to quantify the cost of manual freight reconciliation (template included)"
- "What a 20% reduction in manual labor hours is worth at a 7x EBITDA multiple"
- "The metrics PE firms look at when evaluating operational efficiency — and how to move them"

**Pillar 4 — Behind the Methodology (10% of content)**
Transparent process posts. Shows the sophistication of the WynDelta approach.
- "How we score automation opportunities before recommending anything"
- "Why we run every agent deployment in shadow mode first"
- "The 6-tool engagement system we built to take a supply chain org from assessment to deployed agent"

---

### Content Cadence

| Day | Post Type | Pillar |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Monday | Text post — specific ops observation or insight | Pillar 1 |
| Wednesday | Framework or process breakdown (can include image) | Pillar 2 or 3 |
| Friday | Story-format post — narrative + lesson | Any, rotate |

**Post format philosophy (Hormozi-adjacent):**
- Hook in the first line. No "I've been thinking about..." No "Hot take:". Just the insight.
- Short paragraphs. Each line earns the next.
- End with a question that invites engagement or a CTA to DM.
- No jargon. If a VP Ops at a 40-person food distributor wouldn't immediately understand it, rewrite it.

### Lead Magnet: The ARA Scorecard

A simple interactive tool (can be an HTML page or Google Form) that lets a supply chain operator self-score their top 3 manual processes across 5 dimensions. Output: their Automation Readiness Score + an emailed breakdown of where they stand.

**This does three things:**
1. Generates warm leads who are self-identified as interested in automation
2. Demonstrates WynDelta's methodology before any conversation happens
3. Gives Kevin real prospect data to reference in follow-up conversations

Promote on every content post. Gate with email (not a wall — just enough to start the relationship).

## 6. The Outreach Sequence (Warm DM Follow-Up)

> "Hey [Name] — saw you engaged with my post on [topic]. Curious what's driving the interest — are you actively looking at automation in your ops or more research mode right now?"

**Message 2 (if they reply — value first):**
> "Makes sense. For what it's worth, the most common pattern I see is [specific observation relevant to what they shared]. Sounds like you might be at [stage]. Happy to send over the ARA Scorecard — it takes 10 minutes and gives you a clear picture of where your highest-impact opportunity probably is."

**Message 3 (if they take the scorecard — booking):**
> "Looked at your scores. A couple of things jumped out — [specific observation]. Worth a 30-minute call to talk through it properly. No pitch. I'll map your top opportunity and tell you honestly if it makes sense to go further."
